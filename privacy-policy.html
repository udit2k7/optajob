Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

1. Who We Are

OptaJob is operated by Udit Tandon, based in India. For the purposes of data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Udit Tandon is the data controller responsible for your personal data. You can reach us at optajob.sup@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

2. What We Collect

We collect the following information, stored in our database (Google Firebase Firestore):

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number, job title, and any profile text you enter.
  • Resume content: when you upload a resume, we extract and store the full text of that resume on our servers, so it can sync across your devices and power the app's AI tools. You may store up to 4 resumes, each with a display name and a flag marking your primary resume.
  • AI-generated documents: cover letters and tailored resumes you generate using the app's AI tools are saved to your Portfolio, including their full text, the associated job title and company, and the date created.
  • Saved and unlocked jobs: full job listing details for jobs you save, and the IDs of jobs you have spent credits to unlock.
  • Account activity: your credit balance, daily check-in date, any bonus-day allowance, your selected resume template, whether your email is verified, your account creation date, and the date you last opened the app.
  • Administrative fields: whether your account has admin privileges, your role, and your account status (active/suspended) — these are set only by administrators, never by you.
  • Aggregate usage: we keep a daily count of app opens across all users. This count contains no names, emails, or other identifiers — it is a single number per day.
  • Contact Finder consent: whether you have agreed to use Contact Finder, and when you agreed. We store this so we can prove your choice was recorded and so you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Job search cache: job listings returned by our search providers are stored on our servers for a short period so repeat searches are faster and cheaper. These contain no personal data about you — only public job advertisements.

If you sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account's display name and email address, which become part of the account details above.

3. Why We Collect It, and Our Legal Basis

We collect account details, resumes, saved jobs, and portfolio content because they are necessary to provide the app's core features to you — this is necessary for the performance of our contract with you (our Terms of Service) under GDPR Article 6(1)(b).

We keep administrative fields, account status, and activity logs to secure the platform, prevent abuse of the credit system, and support users who contact us — this rests on our legitimate interests in running a secure, working service under Article 6(1)(f).

Where we ask for your explicit agreement to something beyond what's needed to run the app, we rely on your consent under Article 6(1)(a), and you may withdraw that consent at any time (see Section 8).

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the following parties, and only as needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions) — our infrastructure provider. Firebase stores and processes your account data, resumes, and portfolio content on our behalf, on Google Cloud servers.
  • Job search providers — we use several so that search keeps working if one is unavailable: JSearch (via RapidAPI), Adzuna, Jooble, and OpenWeb Ninja. When you search, only the text of your query (for example, a job title and location) is sent to one of these providers. They do not receive your identity, your resume, or any other account data. We may add or change providers over time.
  • AI providers you choose to use — and an important point about how this works. OptaJob does not run, own, or operate any AI. We do not generate your resume, your cover letter, or your ATS score, and we hold no AI subscription on your behalf. What we provide is the interface and the instructions. The intelligence is yours: you supply your own API key for a provider you select — Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Mistral, Together AI, or a custom endpoint of your choosing — and when you run an AI tool, your resume text and the relevant job description travel directly from your phone to that provider. These requests never pass through OptaJob's servers. We never see the request, we never see the response, and we never store either. Your API key is held in your device's secure storage and is never transmitted to us. In short: OptaJob puts professional-grade AI tools in your hands and points your own AI at the job you're chasing — it does not do the thinking for you or on our behalf.

OptaJob currently displays no advertising of any kind — there is no ad SDK, no ad network, and no advertising cookie in the app. If that changes in the future, we will update this policy before any advertising is introduced.

5. Contact Finder

Contact Finder is an optional tool that helps you reach a hiring manager directly. It is switched off until you explicitly turn it on, and you can switch it off again at any time in Settings.

When you use it, searches for a company's contact details are sent from your device to the contact-data services you have configured with your own API keys — currently Hunter.io, Apollo.io, Snov.io and Anymail Finder. As with our AI tools, these requests go directly from your phone to those services. They do not pass through OptaJob's servers, and we do not receive, see, or store the results.

If those services return nothing, your own AI provider suggests likely email formats instead. These are informed guesses, not verified addresses, and the app labels them as such. They may be incorrect or reach the wrong person.

Any email you send is composed and sent by you, from your own email app on your own device. OptaJob never sends email on your behalf and has no access to your outbox.

Because the people you contact are usually located somewhere other than where you are, you are responsible for following the law that applies to them. Rules on unsolicited business email differ between countries — several, including Germany, Italy, Spain and Sweden, require prior consent even for professional enquiries. Always identify yourself clearly and respect any request to stop contacting someone.

6. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure via Firebase. Google Cloud operates data centers globally, so your data may be stored or processed in a country other than the one you live in, including the United States. Google provides its own safeguards for international transfers as part of its infrastructure; we do not operate separate servers of our own.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your account data, resumes, portfolio content, and activity history for as long as your account remains active. If you ask us to delete your account (see Section 9), we delete the associated data from our database.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct it if it's inaccurate; request its deletion; receive a copy of it in a portable format; object to certain processing; and, where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email optajob.sup@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or EEA and believe we have not handled your data properly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Deleting Your Account and Data

To delete your account and the data associated with it, email optajob.sup@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and request deletion. We will remove your account data from our database.

10. Security

Your data is protected by Firestore security rules that restrict each user's records so that only that user can read or write them. Authorized administrators can view limited account information (such as your name, email, credit balance, verification status, and last-active date) for support and moderation purposes, and can suspend accounts; administrators do not have a built-in feature to read your resume text or portfolio content, though as the data controller, the operator retains underlying technical access to the database. All data in transit between the app and our servers is sent over HTTPS. Data at rest is protected by whatever default storage protections Google Cloud provides as our infrastructure provider — we do not apply additional encryption of our own on top of that.

11. Children

OptaJob is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will also notify users within the app.

13. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of your rights, contact us at optajob.sup@gmail.com.
