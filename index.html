Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OptaJob.

Is OptaJob free to use?

Yes. The app operates on an AI Credit Economy. Every user starts with 3 free credits, plus a daily bonus for your first 30 days. Additional ways to earn credits are in development.

How does the app protect my resume data?

Your AI provider keys are stored in your device's secure storage and are never sent to us — AI requests go directly from your phone to the provider you choose. Your resume text and saved documents are stored in your account on our servers so they sync across your devices; see our Privacy Policy for exactly what we store and how to delete it.

Is OptaJob only for jobs in India?

The job listings we surface are focused on roles based in India — but anyone, anywhere in the world, can download the app and apply. That makes it useful whether you're job-hunting inside India, an Indian professional abroad planning a move home, or an international candidate looking for opportunities in one of the world's fastest-growing job markets. More countries are on the roadmap.

How do I buy credits online?

Our secure web-based payment portal is currently under construction. Once live, you will be able to log in to optajob.com to manage your wallet and purchase fiat packs that instantly sync to your mobile app.
